Join the Ahead beta

Ahead is an iPhone app for people who walk with a white cane. It reads what is at chest and head height with the phone's LiDAR and tells you through vibration, and it answers a spoken question about a photo, which is sent to an AI service to answer. It is in a beta: only a few people have walked with it so far, and it never replaces your cane.

Ahead needs an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor. If you help someone who uses a cane, fill this in for their phone.

Use the email of the Apple Account on the iPhone that will run Ahead; Apple sends the invitation for TestFlight, its free app for trying apps before release, there. If you are helping someone, use their address.

These iPhones have LiDAR: 12 Pro and Pro Max, 13 Pro and Pro Max, 14 Pro and Pro Max, 15 Pro and Pro Max, 16 Pro and Pro Max, 17 Pro and Pro Max. The iPhone Air, the standard models and the SE do not.
