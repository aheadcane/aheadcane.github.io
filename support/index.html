Ahead support

Ahead is in a beta, made by Jonas Neves with a student team. It does not give directions or routes, and it never replaces your cane.

Get help or report a problem

Open a new issue on GitHub. It needs a free GitHub account, and what you write there is public, so leave out your email address and say how you would like to be reached. If you cannot use GitHub, use "Send Beta Feedback" in the TestFlight app, or ask someone you trust to post it for you.

Join the beta

Request an invitation. Ahead needs an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor (12 Pro or a later Pro model; the form lists the models we know of).

Common questions

The app says questions are used up for today. Each phone has a daily number of cloud questions, and the service has a daily total. The vibration and the cane work as usual, and questions return the next day.

The cane does not connect. Turn the cane on, keep it near the phone, and open Settings in Ahead to see the Bluetooth status. The app reconnects by itself.

What does Ahead send out? See the privacy policy.
