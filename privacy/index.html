Ahead privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Ahead is made by Jonas Neves, who is responsible for the data described here.

Ahead is an iPhone app for people who walk with a white cane. This page lists everything Ahead sends from your phone.

When you ask a question

You hold the button and speak. Your speech is turned into text on the phone. Ahead then sends three things to a server run by the maker of Ahead, which passes them to Anthropic's API (the Claude model) to get one spoken sentence back: a photo from the camera (at most 1024 pixels on its longest side), a small grid of distances from the LiDAR sensor, and the text of your question. The server does not save the photo or the question. It does keep ordinary request records (time, IP address, result) that its host, Cloudflare, writes for any web service. Anthropic handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy. A tap without a question never leaves the phone.

When the app first opens

The app makes a random identifier and a cryptographic key pair on the phone. It sends the identifier, the public half of the key, and the name your iPhone gives itself (for example "Maria's iPhone") to the server, which keeps them so it can check that later questions come from the same phone. The private key never leaves the phone. The server also counts each phone's questions per day, to keep the service within its limits, and keeps a one-way hash of your IP address for two hours, to slow down abuse.

If you ask to join the beta

The beta form collects your email address, your iPhone model, whether you use a white cane or help someone who does, and the time you asked. It goes to the server's database, with the name of the page it came from, and, when your iPhone has LiDAR, your email address goes to Apple's TestFlight, which sends the invitation. A request from an iPhone without LiDAR is kept in the database but not sent to Apple. A one-way hash of your IP address is kept for two hours to limit repeated requests. This is kept only to manage the beta. Ask us to delete it and we will; deleting it from our database does not remove you from TestFlight, where Apple holds your address under its own terms, so you also remove yourself there in the TestFlight app.

What stays on the phone

The LiDAR depth, your steps, the vibrations, the speech-to-text, the Bluetooth link to the cane, your settings and the walk log all stay on the phone. The walk log leaves only if you export it yourself. Ahead does not use your location, contacts, calendar, photos library or health data, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your choices

Deleting the app deletes its key and settings from the phone. To have the server's record of your phone, or your beta request, removed, contact us as described on the support page. Beta requests are deleted by email address; a phone's record holds no email address, so tell us the phone's name as it appears in Settings › General › About. Do not post your email address in a public issue: say that you want something deleted and how you are comfortable being reached.
